## Ability and understanding ability

The learner asked what ability and understanding ability mean. The working distinction recorded in [Ability and understanding ability](/knowledge/ability-and-understanding-ability/) is: ability is reliable performance on a type of task; understanding ability is topic-specific reliable use of a model to explain, predict, apply/transfer, and diagnose/revise assumptions. Memory and attention support this work but are not identical to it.

## Understanding words and idioms

The learner asked what it means to understand a word or an **idiom** (interpreting “idom” as “idiom”). The session clarified that a translation/gloss shows recognition, while understanding includes selecting meaning in context, paraphrasing, using or judging use in a new context, distinguishing close alternatives, and repairing an incorrect use. For idioms, the learner must also avoid literal interpretation and recognise the expression's conventional meaning and register. The framework is stored in [Understanding a word or idiom](/knowledge/understanding-words-and-idioms/).
