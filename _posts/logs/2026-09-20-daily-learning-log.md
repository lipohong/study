## Conceptual correction

The learner requested that the workspace be corrected according to the premise: knowledge is the "world model" constructed in the brain.

The workspace now treats external notes, books, videos, data, measurements, and LLM responses as **model resources**, not automatically as the learner's knowledge. Knowledge is the relatively dependable, appropriately supported, usable part of the learner's internal and revisable world model. Facts, concepts, and procedures can be model components; understanding is demonstrated use and revision of a relevant model component; skill and mastery are levels of reliable performance using it.
