## Learner statement (verbatim)

> 學習的本質是破壞與重構：打破舊有的、狹隘的認知，吸收新的資訊，並將其縫合到你原有的知識網路

## Minimal interpretation

The learner requests that learning be represented as an active process of revising an existing world model: locate a narrow, unsupported, or failed relation; take in relevant new information; reorganise the relation with prior knowledge; and test the revised model.

“Destruction” is interpreted narrowly. It does not mean discarding all prior knowledge. It means withdrawing, limiting, or replacing the particular representation, assumption, or relation that conflicts with better evidence or no longer fits its conditions.
