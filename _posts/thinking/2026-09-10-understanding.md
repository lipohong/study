> If a person can recite Newton's second law, `F = ma`, but cannot judge a new situation, do they know it or understand it?

**My answer:**

### 2. Two shopping carts, same applied force

> Cart A is empty and cart B is full. Which has the greater acceleration?

**My answer:**

### 3. B has twice A's mass, same net force

### 9. Cognitive ability versus understanding ability

> A can concentrate and remember well but only recites definitions. B has ordinary memory but explains causal relations and applies concepts to new examples. Who has stronger understanding and cognitive ability?

**My answer:**

> 不知道

**Tutor summary given:**

> B shows stronger demonstrated understanding of the topic because B explains causal relations and applies the concept to new examples. A demonstrates the named cognitive supports—concentration and memory—but definition recall alone does not demonstrate understanding. The short description does not justify ranking either person's overall cognitive ability or intelligence.

### 10. Ability and understanding ability

> 什麼是能力，什麼又是理解能力？

**Tutor summary given:**

> Ability is a reliable capacity to perform a type of task under relevant conditions. Understanding ability is a topic-specific capacity to build and use a model: explain why, predict what changes, apply the idea in a new case, and revise assumptions after counterevidence. Memory and attention can support this performance but are not the same as understanding.
