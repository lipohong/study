## Learner correction (verbatim)

> 請根據 “知識就是大腦中構建的「世界模型」” 修正本workspace的認知

## Minimal interpretation

The learner requests a workspace-wide reframing: knowledge should be centred on the world model constructed in a person's brain, rather than treated primarily as externally stored facts, information, or isolated competences.

This records a requested conceptual correction, not yet independent evidence that the learner can explain the distinction, apply it to a new case, or revise a model after counterevidence.
