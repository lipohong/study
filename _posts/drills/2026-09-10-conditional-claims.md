- **Skill / subskill:** Turn an overgeneralised causal claim into a conditional, testable statement.
- **Why this drill:** It tests whether the learner can move from “many factors exist” to a clear model that states variables, conditions, and a prediction.
- **Starting level:** fragile
- **Timebox / repetitions:** 5–10 minutes; one full response.

## Performance condition

Without receiving a completed answer first, rewrite this claim in your own words:

> 「讀書時間越長，成績一定越好。」

Your rewrite must say what conditions matter. Then give one concrete situation in which merely increasing time would not be expected to improve the result.

## Success criteria

- [ ] Does not use an unconditional word such as “一定” unless the conditions justify it.
- [ ] Names at least two relevant conditions or variables beyond time.
- [ ] States a directional relationship that could in principle be checked with evidence.
- [ ] Gives a counterexample or boundary case that is consistent with the rewritten claim.

## Common error to watch

- Listing many factors without explaining how they change the original conclusion.

## Attempt instructions

1. Write the response first, without asking for the final answer.
2. Save the unedited response under `/practice/attempts/conditional-claims-attempt-01/`.
3. Request feedback against the success criteria.
4. Write the smallest useful correction in a reflection note.

## LLM coach prompt

> Act as a deliberate-practice coach. Do not solve this task before I attempt it. After my attempt, identify the earliest reasoning failure, give one minimal hint, let me retry, then provide a rubric-based review. Update the recommended next drill based on my evidence.

## Next-step rule

- If successful with a mechanism and counterexample: repeat with an unfamiliar domain.
- If partly successful: revise the claim by adding only the missing condition(s).
- If unsuccessful: practise distinguishing correlation, causation, and controlled conditions.
