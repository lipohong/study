> **Use:** compact context that helps an LLM choose the right language, examples, pace, and practice design. Keep this factual and update it from demonstrated evidence.

## Identity and communication

- Preferred language for this workspace: English by default.
- Explanations should be clear, structured, and low-friction. Use Chinese/Cantonese only when the learner requests it or when a short translation removes a real barrier.
- The learner is building an LLM-supported deliberate-practice and self-study system for career, work, study, and life.

## Current environment

- Agent platform: Any Agent, with persistent memory, project context files, session search, tools, and optional scheduled jobs.
- Workspace principle: detailed learning evidence stays in local files; stable high-signal preferences may also live in agentic memory.

## Learning preferences to validate over time

- Wants learning assistance tailored to demonstrated level rather than a generic curriculum.
- Wants reduced *extraneous* cognitive load: clear sequencing, small reasoning steps, consistent terminology, and concrete examples.
- Still wants deliberate practice: attempt before solution, feedback tied to criteria, reflection, and progressive difficulty.
- Values an LLM that can organise prior conversation outcomes into durable, reviewable files.

## Known project context

- The learner is designing this workspace from a notebook outline that separates goals, notes/captures, diagrams, logs, LLM-facing docs, agent instructions, human README, and sources/references.
- The learner also wants the capture taxonomy: **Ideas**, **Reflection**, and **Thinking / Thought**.

## Evidence model

Mark each subskill as one of:

- **secure** — can explain and apply independently;
- **fragile** — partly correct, inconsistent, or needs prompts;
- **missing** — prerequisite or concept is absent;
- **unknown** — not yet assessed.

Do not infer a level from confidence alone. Link evidence to actual attempts, feedback, or teach-backs.
