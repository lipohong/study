## Summary

The video contrasts information with knowledge, defining information as a contextual statement and knowledge as a stable, reusable regularity. It proposes this ladder:

`event -> representation -> rule -> data -> information -> knowledge -> wisdom`

Its running example is cooking a soft-centred egg, progressing from the cooking event through measurements and a table of observations to a heat-transfer relation and a final cooking choice.
