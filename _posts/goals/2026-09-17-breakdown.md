> Turn the current goal into the smallest sequence that produces evidence of progress.

## Active goal

Develop a usable understanding of understanding: distinguish memorisation from model-based reasoning, identify hidden conditions in claims, and apply the framework to unfamiliar cases.

## Phase 1 — Establish current evidence

- [x] Select the first topic: the nature of understanding.
- [x] Gather initial diagnostic evidence through force/mass and supply/demand examples.
- [x] Record the learner's unedited reasoning in a thinking note.
- [x] Confirm the learner's ability to rewrite an overgeneralised causal claim independently, state relevant conditions, and provide a boundary case.
- [ ] Calibrate the revised claim by distinguishing likely effects from necessary/sufficient conditions.

## Phase 2 — Build the model of understanding

- [x] Distinguish remembering a statement from reasoning with a relation under stated conditions.
- [x] Identify that understanding involves more than an explanation that merely sounds fluent.
- [x] Identify that a claim can omit relevant variables or conditions.
- [ ] Practise the working model: explain → predict → apply/transfer → diagnose and revise assumptions.
- [ ] Distinguish cognitive supports (attention, working memory, self-regulation) from understanding ability, and explain how they interact.
- [ ] Test the difference between a correct prediction by pattern-matching and a correct prediction from an explicit model.
- [ ] Distinguish understanding from mastery, and classify the evidence needed for recognition, guided application, independent proficiency, transfer, and teaching/creation.
- [ ] Complete [Practice Drill: Cognitive supports versus understanding](/practice/drills/cognitive-supports-vs-understanding/) without notes.
- [ ] Complete [Practice Drill: Classify understanding and mastery levels](/practice/drills/understanding-and-mastery-levels/) without notes.

## Cognitive support vs. understanding ability

- [ ] Explain the A/B contrast: attention and memory support thinking but do not by themselves demonstrate understanding; explanation and transfer demonstrate topic-specific understanding, while overall cognitive ability remains unmeasured.
- [ ] Practise diagnosing whether a difficulty is caused mainly by missing knowledge/model, cognitive load, or both.

## Phase 3 — Deliberate practice

- [x] Complete [Practice Drill: Conditional causal claims](/practice/drills/conditional-claims/).
- [x] Save an unedited response in [Attempt 01: Conditional causal claims](/practice/attempts/conditional-claims-attempt-01/).
- [x] Receive feedback against the drill criteria.
- [ ] Design a small personal comparison with immediate and delayed outcome measures.
- [ ] Repeat with a new domain (e.g., health, economics, programming, or work decisions).

## Phase 4 — Consolidate

- [ ] Teach back a concise definition of understanding using an original example.
- [ ] Complete a transfer task with an unfamiliar claim.
- [ ] Update `/context/learning-state/` with only high-signal evidence.
- [ ] Create a short review on or before 2026-09-17 and choose the next concept to practise.
