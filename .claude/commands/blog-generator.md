Based on requirement to generate a blog by AI

Usage: /blog-generator $REQUIREMENTS

Please write a new blog according to $REQUIREMENTS.

Please follow `AGENTS.md` as the source of truth. Create the paired English and Traditional Chinese posts directly in the appropriate `_posts/<record-type>/` folder, with the required front matter, one matching top-level category, tags, explicit English/Chinese permalinks, and cross-links. Do not create legacy content folders or a second source copy.

Keep technical terms in English where they improve precision. Do not publish sensitive personal information, credentials, or unapproved copyrighted source material. Use the record's actual event or creation date rather than an arbitrary current timestamp.
